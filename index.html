About the park

A simple place to stay near Mena

We’re a local project putting down roots in Polk County — not a big-box campground chain.

We’re building El Heffe RV Park with a pretty simple idea: a clean, comfortable place to stay without turning camping into something it isn’t.

Near Mena, Arkansas, El Heffe will offer RV sites and a relaxed home base for people visiting the area, riding the trails, exploring the Ouachitas, fishing, traveling through town, or getting away for a while.

We’re about a 10-minute drive from Wolf Pen Gap, and the park is planned as buggy-accessible — so you can ride your side-by-side from your site toward the trails without loading up and trailering it over.

We’re excited to call the Mena area home and look forward to becoming part of the local community.

The Ouachitas

Close to the trails, proud to be in Mena

Wolf Pen Gap

Ride out from the park

El Heffe sits within about a 10-minute drive of Wolf Pen Gap — the trail system people come to this part of the Ouachitas to ride.

Because the park is planned as buggy-accessible, you can keep the side-by-sides handy and head toward the trails from here instead of trailering over for every run.

Mena, Arkansas

A good place to call home

Between the mountains, trails, outdoor recreation, and local businesses, Mena made sense for El Heffe from day one.

We want the park to complement what’s already here — another place to stay that points people toward eating local, shopping local, and exploring Polk County.

Quick answers

FAQ

When do you open?

We’re targeting a soft open in late 2026 or early 2027. That can shift with weather, permits, and construction — join the email list for the real announcement.

Can I reserve a site now?

Not yet. Reservations and rates will go live once sites and utilities are far enough along. We’ll post here and email the list when booking opens.

Will it be ATV / side-by-side friendly?

Yes — that’s a core part of the plan. We’re building toward buggy-accessible stays so you can ride toward Wolf Pen Gap from the park.

Where exactly are you?

Near Mena in Polk County, Arkansas, about 10 minutes from Wolf Pen Gap. Exact address and turn-by-turn directions will be published before opening.

Can food trucks or local businesses partner?

We’re planning a food-truck pad and love the idea of local partners. Reach out through the contact form and tell us what you’ve got.
